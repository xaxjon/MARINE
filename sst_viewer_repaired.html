SST Viewer Instructions

1. Workflow:

  • Click 'Download SST Map' to open the NOAA website.
  • Select your desired chart region and date.
  • Right-click the chart image and select 'Save Image As...' to save it to your device.
  • Return to this viewer and click 'Open Chart' to load the saved file.

2. Calibration: The viewer uses your custom palette data by default.

3. Operation:

  • Pan/Zoom: Drag to move, Scroll to zoom.
  • Inspect: Hover to see Lat/Lon/Temp.
  • Profile: Double-click two points to plot a temperature graph for a fishing run.
  • Enhance: Toggles histogram equalization for better contrast.
